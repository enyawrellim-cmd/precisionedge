Three Modes

One app. Three ways to use it.

PrecisionEdge opens to a mode selector every time. Pick your role and go.

🏋️
Player

Player Mode

Solo shot tracking with AR and GPS. Build your personal club data one shot at a time.

  • AR + GPS carry distance and lateral deviation
  • Club-by-club dispersion patterns
  • AR trajectory overlays on the range
  • 2D dispersion maps and target landing views
  • Smart club distance suggestions
🧠
Coach

Coach Mode

Manage your team, track every player's shots on the range and course, review rounds later.

  • Team roster with up to 12 color-coded players
  • Range sessions with per-player shot tracking
  • Course play with pre-drawn hole templates
  • Save courses and load them instantly
  • Putting green with numbered shot sequence
  • Session history with full round review
⛳️
Range Day

Range Day Mode

Quick-fire team sessions at the range. Pick players, assign clubs, and track shots fast.

  • Streamlined for driving range speed
  • Per-player club assignments
  • Rapid shot logging
  • Session naming and review
Course Play

Build your course in two minutes flat

Coach looks at the back of the scorecard, picks a hole layout and yardage for each hole, and locks in the entire course before the round starts. Every hole loads instantly during play.

18 Pre-Drawn Hole Templates

Smooth bezier-curved fairways, water hazards, bunkers, and greens. Par 3s, par 4s, par 5s, doglegs left and right, water carries, island greens, and double doglegs — all with organic shapes that look like real holes.

Save your course for later. Load it next week without rebuilding. Name your rounds and review them from the dashboard with the same template visuals and shot data overlaid.

Features

Built for golfers who want the truth

No guessing. No vibes. PrecisionEdge uses your phone's AR camera and GPS to capture real data from real shots on real courses.

📡

AR + GPS Shot Capture

Mark your ball, pick your club, hit it, walk to the landing spot. Carry distance and lateral deviation measured automatically.

🎯

Dispersion Patterns

See your real shot spread for every club — in AR trajectory overlays, 2D dispersion maps, and target landing views.

📳

LiDAR Putting

On devices with LiDAR, the putting green comes alive. Precision distance tracking on the green with numbered shot sequences.

👥

Team Management

Up to 12 color-coded players per team. Track every player's shots, assign clubs, and review per-player stats across sessions.

💾

Save Everything

Courses save for reuse. Rounds save with names for future review. Session history lives on the dashboard — tap to replay any round.

🌐

4-Language Support

Full interface in English, Spanish, Japanese, and Korean. Switch languages anytime from any mode.

How It Works

Pick your mode. Go play.

No launch monitor. No sensors. Just your iPhone on the course or range.

Choose your mode

Player for solo practice, Coach to run your team, or Range Day for quick group sessions at the driving range.

Set up your session

Player: pick a club and target distance. Coach: build your course from templates or start a range session. Range Day: assign clubs and go.

Track shots

AR and GPS do the heavy lifting. Tap where the ball lands, and PrecisionEdge calculates carry, deviation, and accuracy.

Review your data

Named sessions save to history. Pull up any round, see shot-by-shot data overlaid on hole templates or dispersion maps.
