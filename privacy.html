01

Overview

PrecisionEdge ("we," "our," or "us") is a golf shot tracking application developed by PrecisionEdge Golf LLC, a Hawaii limited liability company. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

The short version: Your shot data stays on your device. We do not collect personal information, we do not track you, and we do not sell any data to anyone.

02

Data We Collect

PrecisionEdge collects the following data solely to provide its core functionality — tracking your golf shots and building your dispersion patterns:

📍

Location Data

GPS coordinates of your shot start and landing positions, used to calculate carry distance and lateral deviation.

🎥

Camera & AR Data

Camera access for the AR overlay and shot visualization. Images are processed on-device and are not transmitted or stored externally.

⛳

Shot Data

Club selection, target distance, carry distance, lateral deviation, and bearing. All stored locally on your device.

🌐

Course Data

When available, approximate course and hole information is retrieved via a third-party golf course API to assist with distance auto-fill.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Health or fitness data from HealthKit
  • Browsing history, contacts, or photos
  • Any data for analytics, advertising, or tracking purposes
03

How We Use Data

All data collected by PrecisionEdge is used exclusively to:

  • Calculate and display your shot distances and dispersion patterns
  • Render AR visualizations of your shot trajectories
  • Auto-detect the golf course you are playing (via GPS and the course API)
  • Provide distance suggestions based on hole information

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core app functionality.

04

Data Storage & Retention

All shot data is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. No shot data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

Your data persists on your device until you choose to delete it using the in-app data management feature. If you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is removed.

We do not operate servers that store your personal data. There are no user accounts, no cloud sync, and no backups of your data on our systems.

05

Third-Party Services

PrecisionEdge uses the following third-party service:

  • Golf Course API (GolfAPI.io) — Used to identify nearby courses and retrieve hole/distance information. Your approximate GPS location is sent to this service solely to identify the course. No personal information is transmitted. GolfAPI.io's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or social media SDKs.

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

06

Your Rights

Because your data is stored entirely on your device, you have full control over it at all times:

  • Access: View all your shot data within the app at any time
  • Delete: Remove individual shots, all shots for a club, or all data using the in-app management tool
  • Revoke permissions: Disable location or camera access at any time through your device's Settings app
  • Uninstall: Removing the app deletes all associated data from your device

If you have questions about your data or wish to exercise any privacy rights, please contact us using the information below.

07

Children's Privacy

PrecisionEdge is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal information from any user, no special provisions for children's data are necessary.

08
