GPS Accuracy Tips

GPS works best outdoors in clear conditions. Accuracy depends on satellite signal strength and atmospheric conditions.

Why are my shot distances inconsistent?
GPS accuracy varies based on several factors: number of visible satellites, cloud cover, buildings or trees blocking the signal, and the device's GPS chip quality. Most iOS devices have accurate GPS within 5-10 feet. Take shots when you have a clear sky view for best results.
How can I improve GPS accuracy?
Move away from trees, buildings, and structures that block satellite signals. Allow the app a few moments after launching to lock onto satellites — you'll see the GPS indicator at the top of the screen. Play in open areas of the course. Avoid playing during overcast or stormy weather.
What if the app can't lock onto GPS?
Make sure Location Services are enabled for PrecisionEdge in Settings > Privacy > Location Services. Restart the app if it's struggling to lock in. Allow up to 30 seconds for the first GPS fix of the day. If problems persist, restart your device.
Does PrecisionEdge use assisted GPS (A-GPS)?
Yes. The app downloads Wi-Fi and cellular data to help your device lock onto satellites faster. This requires internet access but improves GPS initialization. After the initial lock, the app works offline.

AR Camera Issues

AR requires a compatible device and proper permissions. Most modern iPhones and iPads support ARKit-based features.

Why isn't AR working?
Check that Camera permission is granted for PrecisionEdge in Settings > Privacy > Camera. Make sure your device supports ARKit (iPhone 6s or later, iPad Air 2 or later). AR requires well-lit conditions with visible surface features—avoid low-light or blank walls.
Why do my shots look blurry or shaky in AR?
This usually means the camera can't track the ground surface well. Ensure the app is pointing at the ground with varied texture (grass, dirt, pavement). Move slowly and let the camera stabilize. Avoid pointing straight down or at sky.
How many shots can I visualize in AR?
Up to 25 shots are stored for a round. In the AR view, up to 5 shots are drawn and visible at once. You can cycle through your shots to see different ones in AR.
Does AR require an internet connection?
No. AR rendering happens entirely on your device. You can use AR mode offline.
What's LiDAR putting mode?
LiDAR (available on iPhone 12 Pro/Max, iPhone 13 Pro/Max, and later) provides superior depth sensing for putting greens. The putting mode uses LiDAR to map green topography with centimeter-level precision, helping you visualize slope and read breaks more accurately.

Club Averages

Build club profiles from your own data. The more shots you track, the more accurate your club averages become.

How does PrecisionEdge calculate club averages?
Club averages are calculated from all shots you've logged with each club. The app computes the mean carry distance across all shots with that club. The more shots you log, the more statistically reliable your average becomes.
What are club mismatch warnings?
If you select a club whose average distance doesn't match your target, the app alerts you. For example, if your 7-iron averages 165 yards but the hole is 145 yards away, PrecisionEdge suggests a different club to better match the distance.
How many shots do I need to build a reliable club average?
A good rule of thumb is at least 10-15 shots per club to establish a baseline. 30+ shots gives you a solid statistical profile. Your averages will update automatically as you log more shots.
Can I manually adjust my club averages?
Club averages are calculated automatically from your logged shot data. If you believe an average is inaccurate, review your recent shots with that club or delete outliers to recalibrate.
Can I see my dispersion pattern for each club?
Yes. In the AR view, you can filter shots by club to see how your shots with that club spread across the course. This helps identify consistency issues or bias in one direction.

Subscription & Trial

10-day free trial. No credit card required. Cancel anytime.

Is PrecisionEdge really free for high schools?
Yes. High school coaches can verify their school and receive free access for their team. Verification is done through a simple school email or administrative verification process in the app.
How do I start the 10-day free trial?
When you launch the app, you're automatically enrolled in a 10-day free trial. No credit card is required to start. You'll receive a reminder before the trial ends so you can decide whether to subscribe or cancel.
What happens when my trial ends?
You'll be prompted to subscribe to continue using the app. If you cancel or let your subscription lapse, your shot data remains on your device. You can re-subscribe at any time.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. You can manage and cancel your subscription through the App Store in Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > PrecisionEdge. Cancellation is immediate and effective at the next renewal date.
Will I lose my data if I cancel?
No. Your shot data is stored on your device and will not be deleted if you cancel. You can re-subscribe later and your data will still be there.
What languages does the app support?
PrecisionEdge is available in English, Spanish, Japanese, and Korean. The app will use your device's language setting automatically.

Coach Mode Setup

Coach Mode lets you manage your team's data in one place. Track up to 12 players per team.

How do I enable Coach Mode?
Coach Mode is available to verified coaches. Go to Settings > Coach Mode and follow the verification process. You'll need to provide your school email or administrative credentials. Once verified, you can create a team and add players.
How many players can I add to my team?
Each team can have up to 12 players. If you need to manage more players, you can create additional teams.
How do players join my team?
Players receive a team code or invitation link from you. They enter the code in their own app to join your team. Once joined, their shot data syncs to the team view (with their privacy settings respected).
Can players see each other's data?
Players can see their own data and team aggregate statistics (team averages, trends), but individual player data is private by default. You can adjust visibility settings in Coach Mode to allow peer comparison if desired.
What can I view in Coach Mode?
In Coach Mode you can view: each player's club averages, round summaries, dispersion patterns, progress over time, and team-wide statistics. You can also export data for records or analysis.
Is demo mode available for coaches?
Yes. Demo Mode lets you explore Coach Mode features with sample data without needing a live team. This is useful for training or evaluating the app before rolling it out to your team.
